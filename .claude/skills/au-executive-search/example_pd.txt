POSITION DESCRIPTION
Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

ORGANIZATION: Sustainable Energy Solutions Australia Pty Ltd

LOCATION: Sydney, NSW (with travel to Melbourne and Brisbane offices)

ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION:
Sustainable Energy Solutions Australia (SESA) is a leading renewable energy infrastructure developer and operator in Australia. With over 15 years in the market, SESA develops, owns, and operates utility-scale solar farms, wind farms, and battery storage facilities across Australia. The company currently manages a portfolio of 2.5 GW of installed renewable generation capacity and has a pipeline of 5 GW in development.

SESA is privately held with backing from major Australian superannuation funds and international infrastructure investors. The company employs 450+ people across offices in Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane, with project teams located at operational sites throughout regional Australia.

STRATEGIC CONTEXT:
SESA is entering a transformative growth phase as Australia accelerates its energy transition. The company aims to triple its operational capacity by 2028 and is pursuing both organic development and strategic acquisitions. This growth strategy requires:

- Securing additional project financing ($3-5 billion over the next 4 years)
- Optimizing capital structure and exploring potential IPO pathways
- Integrating acquired assets and development pipelines
- Building financial infrastructure to support scale (systems, processes, talent)
- Navigating regulatory complexity across NEM jurisdictions
- Managing commodity price volatility and grid connection risks

THE ROLE:
Reporting to the Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Executive Leadership Team, the CFO will be the senior financial leader responsible for:

FINANCIAL STRATEGY & CAPITAL MANAGEMENT
- Lead financial strategy to support the company's aggressive growth targets
- Structure and execute project financing for greenfield and brownfield assets
- Manage relationships with lenders, equity investors, and credit rating agencies
- Evaluate and execute M&A opportunities including due diligence and integration
- Assess optimal capital structure and prepare for potential IPO within 18-24 months
- Optimize treasury operations and manage interest rate and FX risk

FINANCIAL OPERATIONS & GOVERNANCE
- Oversee all financial operations including controllership, FP&A, tax, and treasury
- Ensure robust financial controls, audit, and compliance frameworks
- Lead statutory reporting, investor reporting, and board reporting
- Drive financial systems transformation and automation initiatives
- Develop KPI frameworks for asset performance and portfolio optimization

COMMERCIAL & BUSINESS PARTNERING
- Partner with Development and Asset Management teams on project economics
- Lead commercial negotiations for major offtake agreements (PPAs, LGCs)
- Provide financial insights to inform commercial and strategic decisions
- Build financial modeling capability to support investment decisions

LEADERSHIP & CAPABILITY BUILDING
- Build and lead a high-performing finance team of 30+ professionals
- Establish centers of excellence in project finance, FP&A, and financial systems
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and commercial acumen
- Develop finance talent to support growing operational complexity

IMPORTANT CRITERIA (Essential):
- Qualified accountant (CA/CPA) with 15+ years progressive finance leadership experience
- Minimum 5 years as CFO or equivalent senior finance role in a growth-stage business
- Deep expertise in project finance and infrastructure financing
- Proven track record raising debt and equity capital ($500M+)
- Experience managing complex, multi-stakeholder capital structures
- Strong technical accounting skills (AIFRS) and financial control mindset
- Experience leading finance through high-growth phases (3x+ revenue growth)
- M&A experience including due diligence and post-acquisition integration
- ASX listing experience (pre-IPO preparation or as CFO of listed entity) highly regarded

IMPORTANT CRITERIA (Desirable):
- Renewable energy, utilities, or infrastructure sector experience
- Experience with project-level financing structures (limited recourse, tax equity)
- Understanding of Australian energy markets and regulatory frameworks (AEMO, AER)
- Prior experience in private equity-backed businesses
- Track record implementing financial transformation programs (systems, processes)
- Experience managing commodity price risk and hedging strategies

PERSONAL ATTRIBUTES:
- Strategic thinker with strong commercial acumen
- Entrepreneurial mindset comfortable with ambiguity and fast-paced growth
- Exceptional stakeholder management skills (board, investors, lenders, regulators)
- Strong leadership presence and ability to influence at executive and board level
- Collaborative style with ability to build trusted partnerships across the business
- Resilient and adaptable to changing business priorities
- Commitment to sustainability and the energy transition

COMPENSATION:

- Short-term incentive: up to 60% of base (performance-based)
- Long-term incentive: equity participation aligned with company growth
- Total remuneration package: $750,000 - $1,000,000
